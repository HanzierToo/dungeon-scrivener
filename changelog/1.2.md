# v1.2, 2026-09-25

## For players

- Play and portable exports adopt a shared terminal presentation with a dark green palette, monospace text, subtle scanlines, and separate story, input, and inventory panels.

## For authors

- Sage uses a richer code editor, open-file tabs, folder or flat-path views, remembered sorting/view preferences, and path context actions.
- Studio adds a guided game-building tutorial alongside its general tour and mode-specific guides.
- Playtest makes checkpoints and replaying another branch easier to find while keeping the play session separate from authored files.
- Apprentice graph and form behavior receives further interaction and responsive-layout improvements.

## For developers

- Studio's dev/build commands prepare the portable-player artifact automatically.
- Portable-player generation and Studio service-worker caching are updated to support the integrated authoring/reader workflow.
- Browser journeys expand for tours, Sage editing, graph interactions, playtest checkpoints, and ZIP flows.

The [v1.2 follow-up verification record](../docs/release/1.2-follow-up-evidence.md) was produced after this tag and includes later local fixes. Its counts are not presented as results for the immutable v1.2 source.
